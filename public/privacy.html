Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Gatherable, LLC ("Gatherable", "we", "us") collects through gatherablehub.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email galinsky.george@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • When you contact us or book a meeting: your name, email address, company, phone number, whether you work at an agency or a brand, what you need help with, your answers to our follow-up questions, and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • When you use the free Pitch Check: your name, email address, company, role, the type of document and any context you add, and the document you upload. The document itself is sent for analysis and then discarded; we do not store it. We keep the scorecard the analysis produces.
  • When you buy a digital product: your name, email address, what you bought and the amount paid. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our servers record basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We use it to keep the site secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • Analytics: if we turn on Google Analytics, it uses cookies to tell us how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed. We do not use advertising cookies.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you, prepare for and hold meetings, and provide the services you ask for.
  • To produce your Pitch Check scorecard and to prepare a short internal summary of new inquiries. We use an artificial intelligence service to help with both.
  • To deliver products you buy, provide download links and keep records of sales.
  • To follow up about your inquiry or purchase. We will not add you to a newsletter without your permission.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent misuse and improve how it works.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and our business, and only as needed for that work:

  • Render hosts the website.
  • Neon hosts our database in the United States.
  • Anthropic provides the artificial intelligence service that analyses Pitch Check documents and summarizes inquiries. It processes this information under commercial terms that do not allow it to be used to train AI models.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Google provides analytics, if we turn it on.
  • Cal.com or Calendly handles meeting scheduling, if you book a time through the site. Their own privacy policies apply to what you enter there.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and Pitch Check information for as long as it is useful for our relationship with you, and generally no longer than three years after our last contact. We keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Documents uploaded to the Pitch Check are not kept at all. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, including California and other US states, you may have additional rights under local law. To make a request, email galinsky.george@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days and will not treat you differently for making a request.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the site, store sensitive settings in encrypted form, and limit access to personal information to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

This website is for businesses and professionals. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
