How we work

Analytical precision. Human imagination.

Our method builds on the first formal model of creative thought, mapped by Graham Wallas in 1926, and pairs it with modern AI-driven analysis. Human insight leads; technology amplifies.

STEP 01

Assembly

Wallas: Preparation

Gather the right inputs and the right people around your challenge.

STEP 02

Examination

Wallas: Incubation

Probe, prompt and question the questions to find what actually matters.

STEP 03

Distillation

Wallas: Illumination

Turn findings into a sharp position, story and recommendation.

STEP 04

Validation

Wallas: Verification

Test it with data and stakeholders so the result is measurable.

The outcome: communications that make sense and can be measured, turning ideas into changed perceptions, desired behaviors and more transactions.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

What exactly does Gatherable do?

We help advertising agencies win more business and keep more clients: stronger pitches, RFP responses and credentials presentations, better client relationships, and sharper strategy and creative. We also help brands find and choose the right agency.

Can you coach our team before a pitch?

Yes. We join your live rehearsals and give evaluation, advice and coaching on both content and delivery before you present.

Can you repair a strained agency–client relationship?

Yes, and we have done it. We provide independent research, advice and practical solutions to rebuild trust, deepen commitment and improve understanding on both sides.

Do you have agency affiliations? Is the search impartial?

We have trusted creative, media, PR, search and production partners. In an agency search they are included only if they fit the brief, any relationship is disclosed up front, and we never take referral fees from any agency, for any reason.

How are engagements priced and scoped?

Every engagement is customized, from a single deck review to a full agency search or ongoing collaboration. Tell us your challenge and we'll recommend the right scope.

Do you work outside Connecticut?

Yes. Our roots are in Connecticut and New England, and we work with agencies and brands in New York and beyond.
